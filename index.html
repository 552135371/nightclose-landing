Frequently asked questions

How is this different from Streaks or Done?

We don't track consecutive days. NightClose uses a 42-day cycle—21 completions in 42 days. You can skip days without guilt. And when you can't do it, we give you a quiet exit, not a lecture.

What if I miss more than half the days?

The cycle resets, but your total progress stays. No shame, no punishment—just a fresh start. We're betting on consistency over perfection.

Why "win-only" timeline?

Because staring at red Xs makes you quit. Your brain needs to see progress, not failure. We show only your wins—AI analyzes the rest behind the scenes.

Is this a therapy app?

No. We're a habit tracker for people who need emotional support, not clinical help. If you're struggling with mental health, please see a professional.
